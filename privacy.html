Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how our billiard applications collect, use, and manage user data.

Information We Collect

Our applications may collect player names, game scores, and tournament statistics to provide core app functionality, such as leaderboards and scorekeeping.

Google Sheets Integration

Users may choose to connect their Google Sheets accounts to sync player data and statistics. We only access the spreadsheets authorized by the user to read and write app-related data. We do not store or share your Google account credentials or unauthorized drive files.

External Payment Links

Our application may provide links to external third-party payment processors. We do not collect, process, or store financial credentials or payment card details within the app. All payment transactions take place entirely on those external platforms and are subject to their respective privacy policies.

Third-Party Services and Advertising

We use third-party services like Google AdSense to serve advertisements. These services may use cookies, IP addresses, and standard web device identifiers to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can manage or opt out of ad personalization via your Google Ad Settings.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy or your data, please contact us via our Contact page.
